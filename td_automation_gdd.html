Game Overview
// CORE CONCEPT & PILLARS
// CONCEPT SUMMARY IRONFRONT is a hybrid tower defense + factory automation game. Waves of enemies advance on your base — but you don't just place towers and wait. You build a live resource economy using conveyers, machines, and extractors that continuously fund and power your defenses. The tension comes from splitting attention: optimizing your factory pipeline while managing an active assault.

Core Pillars

⚔ ACTIVE DEFENSE
Place and upgrade towers to stop enemy waves. Tower placement, range, synergies, and targeting priority all matter. No idle watching — constant adaptation is required.
tower defensestrategy
⚙ FACTORY AUTOMATION
Build conveyor networks, extractors, and processing machines to generate resources passively. Your factory runs in parallel with the defense — neglect it and you'll run dry.
automationeconomy
🔬 DEEP UPGRADES
Every tower has a multi-tier upgrade tree. Specialize snipers into railguns or EMP bursters. Evolve your LMG into a minigun fortress. No two runs look the same.
progressionreplayability
🛰 OTL SUPERWEAPONS
Orbit-to-Land weapons (laser, missile, nuke, metal beam) are rare, resource-hungry, and devastating. Manage orbital cooldowns as your last line of defense.
superweaponorbital

Map Layout

// IDEA The map has two distinct zones:
  • FACTORY ZONE — rear area where you lay down your automation infrastructure. Enemies don't reach here unless your front collapses.
  • DEFENSE ZONE — enemy paths and tower placement area. Resource nodes scattered throughout create trade-offs.

Win / Lose Conditions

  • ❌ LOSE — Enemies reach your core and deplete its HP to 0
  • ✅ WIN — Survive all waves including the final boss wave
  • ⭐ BONUS — Optional objectives like "core never hit" or "no OTL used" for extra rewards
// DESIGN NOTE Core HP being a limited resource (not just one-shot-lose) adds drama and comeback mechanics.

Automation System
// FACTORY · CONVEYORS · MACHINES
// CORE PHILOSOPHY The automation system is what separates IRONFRONT from typical tower defense. Building an efficient factory is as important as tower placement. A good economy lets you spam upgrades; a bad one leaves you defenseless by mid-game.

Automation Components

⛏ EXTRACTOR
Placed on a resource node. Automatically mines ore, wood, crystals, or other materials at a set rate. Upgradeable for speed and yield.
inputpassive
📦 CONVEYOR BELT
Transports items from one machine to another. Can be split, merged, filtered, and upgraded for speed. The backbone of your factory.
transportfilterable
🔧 PROCESSOR / SMELTER
Converts raw materials into refined goods. Iron Ore → Iron Bars → Steel. Processing chains unlock better tower materials.
refinechain
🏭 ASSEMBLER
Combines multiple refined materials into tower components or upgrades. The endpoint of most factory chains. Can be parallelized for throughput.
craftingendpoint
🔋 POWER GENERATOR
Machines require power. Generators burn fuel to produce power. Upgrading generators or switching to solar/crystal power unlocks better efficiency.
powerfuel
🗄 STORAGE UNIT
Buffer storage for materials. Prevents factory backups. Smart storage can auto-route materials based on demand signals from assemblers.
buffersmart routing
🔀 SPLITTER / MERGER
Splits one conveyor lane into two (or more) — or merges multiple belts. Essential for balancing production. Can be set to priority modes.
belt logic
🤖 DRONE COURIER
Aerial transport unit that bypasses terrain. More expensive than belts but routes freely over obstacles and defense structures.
aeriallate-game

Escalation System

EARLY WAVES (1–5)
Grunts and Rushers only. Prep time is generous. Focus on getting your first factory chain running.
DIFFICULTY
MID WAVES (6–12)
Armored units, flyers, and first Sappers appear. Factory defense becomes important. Prep time shortens.
DIFFICULTY
LATE WAVES (13–18)
Tanks, Jammer Drones, Carriers. Multi-lane assaults. Economy pressure is intense. OTL weapons become necessary.
DIFFICULTY
ENDGAME (19–20)
Void Walker + max-tier enemies. All enemy types simultaneously. Factory likely under constant attack. This is the final test.
DIFFICULTY
// IDEA: DYNAMIC WAVES Instead of purely scripted waves, consider a budget system — each wave gets an enemy-budget that the game fills with a mix of units based on the player's current tower composition. Weak to aerial? More flyers spawn. Heavy defense? More Sappers. Creates adaptive difficulty.

Towers & Combat

TOWER SYNERGIES
Certain towers placed adjacent buff each other — Bola + Shotgun = devastating slowed spread. Creates placement puzzles.
TOWER DECAY
Towers degrade over time and need maintenance from the factory. Adds urgency to the automation loop.
HERO UNIT
A controllable hero character who can manually fight, repair machines, or place towers. Adds an action layer on top of strategy.
WALL SYSTEM
Buildable walls that redirect enemy paths through your kill zones. Can be upgraded to electric walls or spike walls.

Automation & Factory

UNDERGROUND BELTS
Tunnel belts that go under paths, allowing factory routing under enemy lanes without conflict.
OVERFLOW MARKETS
Excess factory output can be sold for gold, which can buy rare resources you can't mine. Trade economy as a late-game money tap.
MACHINE TIERS
Mk1 → Mk2 → Mk3 machines. Higher tiers process faster and can handle more complex recipes. Require more power.

Game Modes

ENDLESS MODE
No final boss — waves keep coming with increasing difficulty. Score tracked. Leaderboards.
PUZZLE MODE
Fixed resource budget, pre-set enemy waves. Solve the optimal factory + tower layout. Like a puzzle game skin over the core systems.
CO-OP
2-player: one player manages towers, other manages the factory. Forces communication and coordination. Very high tension.

More Tower Ideas

FLAMETHROWER
Short range, continuous fire cone. Burns ground for lingering area damage. Strong vs swarm, weak vs armored.
TESLA COIL
Chains lightning between nearby enemies. Better the more clustered the group. Synergy with Net/Bola.
POISON GAS MORTAR
Fires canisters that create lingering gas clouds. Doesn't kill fast but denies areas. Utility tower.
MAGNET TOWER
Pulls metallic enemies toward it, clustering them. Works as a bait + area damage combo with nearby explosive towers.
SPEAR LAUNCHER
Fires large spears that pin enemies to the ground, immobilizing them for a duration. High single-target CC.
ANTI-AIR MISSILE BAT
Dedicated anti-air tower. Fires tracking missiles at aerial units. Useless vs ground. Placement trade-off.
