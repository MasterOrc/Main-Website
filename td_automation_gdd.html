Game Overview
// CONCEPT · PILLARS · LOOP
// CONCEPTIRONFRONT is a tower defense + factory automation hybrid. Enemies advance along fixed paths on BTD6-style prebuilt maps. Players simultaneously build and manage a live resource-production factory to fund and supply defenses. The more you automate, the stronger your arsenal — but the factory needs space, power, and protection.

Core Pillars

⚔ ACTIVE DEFENSE
BTD6-style prebuilt maps with fixed enemy paths and defined tower slots. Tower synergies, placement, and targeting priorities all matter. No passive watching — constant adaptation required.
tower defenseplacement
⚙ FACTORY AUTOMATION
Extract, process, and assemble materials in a real logistics chain. Machines and conveyors run in parallel with the defense. A broken supply line means no upgrades and no repairs.
automationeconomy
🚛 REALISTIC LOGISTICS
Resources physically travel via delivery trucks. Truck depots pick up materials from storage and deliver to tower supply points. Bottlenecks are physical and spatial. Inspired by real supply chains.
logisticstrucks
🎮 FLEXIBLE MODES
Six distinct modes — pure TD intro, factory-only tutorial, full hybrid, hardcore, realistic simulation, and endless. Each has multiple difficulty levels with independent settings.
modesdifficulty

Map Structure

  • ENEMY PATHS — Fixed routes, BTD6-style. Visible to player before wave starts.
  • TOWER ZONES — Defined grid slots alongside paths for tower placement.
  • FACTORY ZONE — Rear area for automation infrastructure. Vulnerable to Sappers and Bombers.
  • ROADS — Pre-existing truck routes in factory zone. Expandable with road-laying tools.
  • RESOURCE NODES — Scattered across both zones. Nodes near the defense line create strategic trade-offs.

Player Health

  • Players have a set base HP — not a one-hit loss condition.
  • Different enemy types deal different HP amounts on reaching the core.
  • HP pool varies per game mode and difficulty — Easy has generous HP, Extreme has minimal.
  • HP can be partially restored between waves using resources (at a cost).
// BONUS OBJECTIVESPer-wave achievements like "Zero damage taken", "No OTL used", "All enemies cleared in under 60s" give bonus loot and meta-progression currency.

Game Modes
// SIX MODES · DIFFICULTY SCALING · STARTING CONDITIONS
// DESIGN GOALEvery mode should feel meaningfully different — not just easier or harder, but a genuinely different way to play. Starting money differs per mode and difficulty. New players enter through tutorial modes; veterans push into Hardcore and Realistic.

The Six Modes

🏗 FACTORY ONLY
Pure automation tutorial. No combat input needed — supply a pre-existing defense installation. Waves come but pre-placed towers handle them. Teaches the entire factory system with low stakes.
tutorialbeginnerfactory focus
🗼 TOWER ONLY
Pure tower defense — no factory needed. Resources auto-generate on a timer. Teaches all tower types, synergies, and wave behavior in a classic TD style. A good entry point for TD veterans.
no factoryclassic TD
⚙ STANDARD
The full IRONFRONT experience. Both factory and defense active. Scripted waves on easier difficulties, partially dynamic on harder. The primary mode with all systems enabled.
full gameall systems
💀 HARDCORE
Standard mode with tower decay, reduced starting money, low HP, shorter prep windows, and mostly-dynamic waves. Losing a key tower mid-wave can cascade catastrophically. No margin for error.
tower decaylow HPdynamic waves
🔬 REALISTIC
Deep simulation. Towers wear down and need repair drones. Higher-tier machines are more power-efficient. Real logistics constraints apply. Fully dynamic waves. Hardest base difficulty in the game.
simulationrepair dronespower efficiency
♾ ENDLESS
Separate mode — no predefined final wave. Waves escalate infinitely. Bosses appear periodically. No end boss unless the player manually triggers "Final Stand" to end their run on their own terms. Score tracked on leaderboards.
infiniteleaderboardfinal stand

Automation System
// FACTORY · MACHINES · POWER · TIERS
// DESIGN PHILOSOPHYThe factory should feel realistic — raw materials enter, get processed through multiple stages, and exit as usable components. Power generation is critical infrastructure. Without power, machines stop. Without machines, the defense stagnates.

Factory Components

⛏ EXTRACTOR
Placed on a resource node. Mines at a set rate. Upgrade for speed and yield. Requires power to operate.
inputpassive
📦 CONVEYOR BELT
Transports items between machines. Upgradeable for speed. Splitters, mergers, and filter modules allow intelligent routing.
transportfilterable
🔧 SMELTER / PROCESSOR
Converts raw materials to refined goods. Iron Ore → Iron Bar → Steel. Multi-stage chains unlock advanced tower materials.
refiningchain
🏭 ASSEMBLER
Combines refined materials into tower components or upgrade parts. The endpoint of most factory chains. Parallelizable for throughput.
craftingendpoint
🔋 POWER GENERATOR
Burns fuel (coal, oil) to power nearby machines. Essential from day one. Upgrade to solar, nuclear, or crystal generators.
powerfuel
🗄 STORAGE UNIT
Buffer storage preventing factory backups. Smart routing option auto-directs materials based on downstream assembler demand.
buffersmart routing
🔀 SPLITTER / MERGER
Splits one belt lane into two, or merges multiple lanes. Priority modes direct overflow intelligently.
belt logic
🏪 OVERFLOW MARKET
Sell excess factory output for gold. Sell only — no buying. Prevents economy bypass while giving a useful outlet for surplus materials.
sell onlygold income

Logistics System
// DELIVERY TRUCKS · DEPOTS · UPGRADE TIERS
// CORE CONCEPTResources don't teleport to towers. Delivery trucks physically travel between Truck Depots (in the factory zone) and Tower Supply Points. The speed, capacity, and route efficiency of your logistics network is as important as the factory itself.

How Delivery Works

  • Trucks follow designated road paths on the map. Roads can be extended with a road-laying tool.
  • Multiple depots on the same storage increase throughput (parallelism).
  • Trucks can be priority-flagged — set a tower as high-priority and trucks will service it before others.
  • If a depot is destroyed by Sappers or Bombers, deliveries on that route halt until repaired.
  • Traffic can back up — good road network planning matters.

Maps
// PREBUILT · BTD6-STYLE · OWN DIFFICULTY RATING
// MAP PHILOSOPHYMaps are prebuilt and fixed — like BTD6. Enemy paths are defined. Tower placement zones are defined. The factory zone is behind the defense line. Each map has its own independent difficulty rating separate from the game mode difficulty setting. Large maps so both defense and factory zones feel spacious.

Map Zones

  • ENEMY PATH(S) — Fixed routes from entry to core. Some maps have multiple converging paths.
  • TOWER ZONES — Defined grid slots around the path for tower placement.
  • FACTORY ZONE — Rear area for machines, extractors, and depots. Accessible to Sappers and Bombers.
  • ROADS — Pre-existing truck routes in factory zone. Expandable with road-laying tool.
  • RESOURCE NODES — Scattered across both zones. Nodes near the defense line create risk/reward trade-offs.

Map Difficulty Ratings

EASYWide paths, many tower slots, large factory zone, short path length.
MEDIUMStandard pathing, normal slot count, some factory obstacles.
HARDMultiple entry paths, tight tower slots, constrained factory area.
EXTREMEBranching paths, minimal tower slots, resource nodes near combat, limited factory space.

Co-op & PvP
// MULTIPLAYER MODES

Co-op Mode 1 — Open Play

// OPEN CO-OPAny player can do anything — place towers, build factory, manage logistics. Best for friends who want to work together without strict role division. No restriction on 2+ players.

Co-op Mode 2 — Divided Command

// SPLIT ROLEPlayers choose one of two roles before the map starts. At least one player must be on each side. Supports 2+ players — extra players join either side freely.
⚔ DEFENSE COMMANDER
Responsible for all tower placement, targeting priority, upgrades, and OTL weapon deployment. Cannot touch the factory — must communicate resource needs to the Factory Engineer.
towersOTLtargeting
⚙ FACTORY ENGINEER
Responsible for all extraction, factory chains, logistics (trucks, routes), and overflow selling. Cannot place or upgrade towers — must deliver the right materials to keep the Defense Commander funded.
factorylogisticsresources
// DESIGN NOTEDivided Command creates a communication meta-game. "I need 200 Steel for the Sniper upgrade" vs "I can't produce Steel until the Smelter is powered — where's the coal?" The tension and dependency IS the game mode.

PvP — Attack & Defend

// PVP MODETwo players (or teams) each have their own map mirror. Both simultaneously build defense AND produce attack units to send at the opponent's base. Kills and wave completions earn Attack Credits used to buy and send enemy units directly onto the opponent's path.
YOUR SIDE
Build defense and factory normally. Survive the waves your opponent sends while sending waves back.
ATTACK CREDITS
Earned from kills and efficient factory runs. Spend in the Attack Shop to send Rushers, Tanks, Bombers, or boss-tier units to the opponent's path.
WIN CONDITION
Reduce the opponent's base HP to zero. Can also win by surviving all natural waves if the opponent runs out of HP first.
